Portfolio

Graphics Campaigns

Three campaigns, each with its own visual system across video graphics, presentation slides, social posts, and course pages.

Data 101

Concept project: lecture video graphics and Canvas course pages for an online class

A matching set of lecture video graphics and Canvas pages for an online Data 101 class. The videos get a title card and lower thirds for the instructor and key terms. The Canvas course uses design-tool layouts for the home page, a module overview, a lecture page with the embedded video and key terms, and a discussion assignment with a rubric. Graph-paper texture, highlighter accents, and clear serif headings carry across the videos and the course site.

Harbor & Pine

Concept project: launch campaign for a coworking brand

A launch campaign for a coworking company opening its second location: keynote slides and social posts for the grand-opening invitation, weekly programming, a founding-member offer, workshops, and hiring. A restrained pine-and-brass palette, a confident serif, and an arched-doorway motif keep it professional and welcoming.

Rise & Rye

Concept project: fall menu campaign for a neighborhood bakery

A seasonal campaign for a neighborhood bakery: a staff kickoff slide, lower thirds for a behind-the-scenes video series, and social posts for the bake schedule, online preorders, a featured item, a loyalty card, hiring, and a kids’ class. Bold, warm type and simple bread illustrations give it a friendly, handmade feel.

AI-Enabled Video Workflows

AI handles the slow first passes of production, so more time goes into story, pacing, and polish. The editorial decisions stay with me.

Timeline assembly

Interviews and lectures are transcribed automatically. I edit the transcript to pull selects, cut filler and dead air, and assemble a first rough cut, so hours of footage become a working timeline in minutes.

AI-generated footage

When filming isn't practical, I generate short b-roll from text prompts, such as establishing shots, abstract backgrounds, and concept visuals. Each clip is color-matched and trimmed to sit naturally alongside the filmed footage.

AI-generated imagery

I create custom stills for thumbnails, title cards, slide backgrounds, and lower thirds, and use generative fill to extend or clean up photos so they fit any aspect ratio.
