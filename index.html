I bring experience as a supervisor, educator, and creative communicator, and I've developed a genuine passion for digital design and video production. Throughout my work in both professional and non-profit settings, I've led teams, supported diverse communities, and created clear, visually engaging materials — from print and digital graphics to fully produced videos — that help people feel informed, connected, and cared for.

As an educator, I learned how to guide others through challenging situations with patience and empathy. I enjoyed designing instructional and informational materials, including video content, that made learning accessible and helped families stay supported. In additional service roles, I worked closely with economically disadvantaged populations, ensuring their needs were met with dignity and compassion.

My experience also includes coordinating non-profit initiatives, managing volunteers, and overseeing communication efforts to ensure outreach was both effective and meaningful. As part of that work, I've handled every stage of video production — recording, editing, encoding, and final delivery — and I use motion graphics and dynamic typography to communicate not just information, but a story. Serving my community is incredibly important to me, and I continue that work through my faith community, where I support the emotional and practical needs of young children.

Outside of work, I love being outdoors — camping, exploring new places, and taking on activities that push me to grow. Those experiences inspire my creativity and shape the way I approach digital design, video editing, and leadership: with resilience, curiosity, and a commitment to helping others succeed.

Portfolio

Graphics Campaigns

Three concept campaigns for fictional organizations. Each one carries its own visual system across presentation slides, video lower thirds, and social posts.

Harbor & Pine

Concept project: launch campaign for a fictional coworking brand

A launch campaign for a coworking company opening its second location: keynote slides, video lower thirds, and social posts for the grand-opening invitation, weekly programming, a founding-member offer, workshops, and hiring. A restrained pine-and-brass palette, a confident serif, and an arched-doorway motif keep it professional and welcoming.

Rise & Rye

Concept project: fall menu campaign for a fictional neighborhood bakery

A seasonal campaign for a neighborhood bakery: a staff kickoff slide, lower thirds for a behind-the-scenes video series, and social posts for the bake schedule, online preorders, a featured item, a loyalty card, hiring, and a kids’ class. Bold, warm type and simple bread illustrations give it a friendly, handmade feel.

Larkspur University, DATA 101

Concept project: lecture video graphics for a fictional university’s online course

A graphics package for an online course’s lecture videos: a lecture title card, lower thirds for the instructor and key terms, and course posts for enrollment, weekly tasks, an instructor introduction, lecture recaps, office hours, and quiz reminders. Graph-paper texture, highlighter accents, and clear serif headings keep the focus on learning.
